# User stories

What each kind of user needs from SchoolPay. Stories are marked ✅ when built.

## Proprietor (school owner)
- ✅ Sign my school up online, confirm my email, and get a checklist of what to set up next.
- ✅ See our plan, what a term costs at our pupil numbers, where to pay, and our SchoolPay bills (PDF).
- ✅ Connect the school's own bank account so all payments settle there (the platform never holds our money).
- ✅ Invite staff by email, choose their role, rename them, change their role, deactivate or reactivate them.
- ✅ Reset a staff member's two-factor sign-in when they lose their phone.
- ✅ See an activity log of every sign-in and change in the school, which nobody can edit.
- ✅ See billed vs collected vs outstanding for the current term at a glance, for any term.
- ✅ See collections per class and per day, how parents pay, and the total owed by debtors.
- ✅ See how late the debts are (not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days) and what's still owed from earlier terms.
- ✅ Trust that the numbers are right without asking the bursar: every figure comes from the same ledger as invoices and receipts.
- ✅ Download the debtors list and money received as Excel or PDF to share with the board.

## Bursar
- ✅ When taking cash or a teller, note which fees it’s for, so it goes to those fees (even if older debts are owed).
- ✅ Add a fee to a class in the fee grid after the bills have gone out, and add it to those pupils’ bills with one click (exempt pupils and optional fees left out).
- ✅ See each pupil’s fees for the term one by one (charged, paid, left) on the pupil’s page, plus earlier debts.
- ✅ (Proprietor) Set the school up on Bachs through Bachs’ own onboarding, beside Paystack.
- ✅ Download every pupil’s fees for a term, for the whole school or one class, each fee marked paid, part paid or unpaid with what’s left (Excel with a sheet per class, or PDF to print).
- ✅ Switch on phone calls for parents who prefer them, with a cap per bill, and mark those parents (and their language) on each pupil's page.
- ✅ Copy a parent's family payment link to send by hand, or replace it if it went to the wrong person.
- ✅ Sign in safely with a password and a code from my phone.
- ✅ Set what each class pays per term in one grid, copy last term's fees, and mark fees like the bus as optional.
- ✅ Set up discounts: automatic sibling discounts, and scholarships or staff-child discounts I give to chosen students.
- ✅ Generate the whole term's invoices in one click, after previewing the total per class; arrears from earlier terms stay owed.
- ✅ Add a charge or correct an invoice without losing the history, and void an invoice issued by mistake.
- ✅ Record what a student owed before we started using SchoolPay.
- ✅ See billed, paid and outstanding for the term, and every invoice's balance.
- ✅ Have bank transfers matched to students automatically: each student has their own account number, and a transfer shows as paid within seconds.
- ✅ Record cash, teller, POS and direct-transfer payments, approved by a second person before they count.
- ✅ See every payment and which invoices it paid, oldest debt first; overpayments are kept as credit and used on the next invoice.
- ✅ Send a parent a card payment link.
- ✅ Reverse a payment recorded in error (e.g. a bounced teller) without deleting anything.
- ✅ Every payment gets a numbered receipt (no gaps) that I can download as a PDF.
- ✅ Keep a debtors list that's always current: filter by class, amount or overdue, sort by most owed or longest overdue, call the parent from the list, and download it for Excel or to print.
- ✅ Report money received for any period, by day and by method, with the full payment list in Excel.
- ✅ Add a fee to some classes after the term's bills have gone out (WAEC, NECO, JAMB, an end-of-year party), leaving out pupils who aren't taking part.
- ✅ Exempt one pupil from a class fee; it comes off their bill, and billing again won't put it back.
- ✅ Keep a price list for things only some pupils buy (uniforms, textbooks) and add them to a pupil's bill with a quantity.
- ✅ Agree with a parent to pay a bill in instalments: reminders follow the instalment dates, and they aren't counted as a debtor while they keep to the plan.
- ✅ Send polite reminders without phoning every parent: reminders go automatically before and after the due date on a schedule I set, stop as soon as the bill is paid, and never exceed a cap per bill.
- ✅ Tell every parent about their new bill when I generate the term's invoices.
- ✅ Send one parent a reminder right now from the student's page.
- ✅ Write the messages in our own words, with a preview, or keep the standard wording.
- ✅ See every message sent to parents: when, how (WhatsApp, text or email), or why it wasn't sent.

## School admin
- ✅ Do everything the bursar does (bills, extra fees, payments, approvals, reversals, reports, messages), with two-factor sign-in; only the proprietor manages staff and the school’s bank account.
- ✅ See the school at a glance on the dashboard: pupils on roll, who joined or left this term, pupils per class and arm, and which pupils still need a class, a parent, consent or an account number (each a link to those pupils).
- ✅ Keep the school's profile (name, address, phone, email) up to date.
- ✅ Set up the session and its three terms, and say which term is current.
- ✅ Set up classes and arms in promotion order, and retire ones no longer used.
- ✅ Load the student list from the school's existing spreadsheet (Excel or CSV), see every problem by row number before saving, and re-import safely.
- ✅ Add a student by hand with their parent, change their class, withdraw or reinstate them.
- ✅ Link parents to children; brothers and sisters share one parent record.
- ✅ Stop reminders for a parent who asks.
- ✅ Give a parent a copy of their data, and erase a former pupil's or parent's details when asked, without breaking the accounts.
- ✅ Move everyone into the next term, or promote them a class at the start of a new session (final-year students graduate), with a preview first.

## Parent
- ✅ Pay for a particular fee (say WAEC) rather than the bill in general, by ticking it on the payment page; the receipt says what it was for.
- ✅ See what each child’s balance is made of (tuition, levies, WAEC, bus…), with what’s paid and what’s left on each, on my portal and on the payment page.
- ✅ If I find messages hard to read, get a phone call in English or Pidgin that tells me what's owed and reads my child's account number slowly, twice, and another when my payment arrives.
- ✅ Open one link from my bill or reminder, no sign-in, and see all my children's balances and account numbers.
- ✅ Pay for several children in one payment, by bank transfer, card or USSD, choosing how much goes to each (part now, the rest later), with a receipt per child: from my link, or "Pay for all your children" when signed in.
- ✅ Pay by card from a link the school sends, and see straight away whether it went through.
- ✅ Sign in with a phone number and a one-time code (no password to forget).
- ✅ See all their children at the school in one place, with what's owed and any credit.
- ✅ Pay by bank transfer to each child's own account number (copy it with one tap), or by card.
- ✅ See each bill's details and every payment, and download receipts any time.
- ✅ Choose the school when I have children at more than one.
- ✅ Use it comfortably on a phone.
- ✅ Get a receipt by WhatsApp (or text, or email) as soon as a payment is received.
- ✅ Get a message when a new bill is ready, and a reminder if it's still owed, never at night.
- ✅ See my instalment plan, what's paid and what's next, and be reminded about the next instalment, not the whole bill.
- ✅ Turn off bill messages and reminders (receipts still come); the school can also do this for me.
- ✅ Download everything the school holds about me and my children, and read a plain privacy notice.
- ✅ Have my details erased once my children have left.

## Anyone with a receipt
- ✅ Check that a receipt is genuine on a public page (scan its QR code), without seeing the child's details. A reversed payment shows as reversed; a fake receipt doesn't match anything.

## Platform admin
- ✅ Switch the payment provider (Paystack or Bachs) for new payments at once, without a release; payments under way and schools not yet set up on the new provider keep working.
- ✅ Create a school and invite its proprietor.
- ✅ Suspend a school (its staff are signed out at once) and reactivate it, with the reason recorded.
- ✅ See platform-level activity (school changes, platform sign-ins, failed sign-ins for unknown emails).
- ✅ See every school at a glance: active, waiting to confirm, suspended, new sign-ups, pupils, money parents paid through SchoolPay, and subscription income owed and overdue.
- ✅ Set the prices (set-up fee, yearly licence, per pupil per year billed termly) and move a school to another plan.
- ✅ Bill every school for the term in one click, after a preview; never bill a school twice for a term.
- ✅ Record a school's bank transfer against its bill, void a bill made in error, and download any bill as PDF.
- ✅ Look a school, staff member, payment, account number or receipt up when someone calls for help, without seeing children's details.
- ✅ See one school's staff (invited, two-factor, last sign-in), figures and bills on one page.

## Every staff member
- ✅ Accept an invite by choosing my own password.
- ✅ Reset a forgotten password by email.
- ✅ Change my password (my other devices are signed out).
- ✅ Turn on two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, and keep recovery codes for a lost phone.
- ✅ Stay signed in across page reloads, and be signed out everywhere when my access is removed.

## Anyone
- ✅ Read what SchoolPay does, how it works and what it costs on the website, and start a sign-up.

## Platform (built)
- ✅ The API reports liveness and readiness for monitoring.
